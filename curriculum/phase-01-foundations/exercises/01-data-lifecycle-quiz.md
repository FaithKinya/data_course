## Questions

1. A marketing team wants yesterday's ad spend by campaign in their dashboard every morning at 8am. Is this batch or streaming? What stages does the data pass through?

2. Why should you keep a raw copy of ingested data instead of only storing transformed tables?

3. Your pipeline writes CSV files that grow to 50GB. What format would you switch to and why?

4. Name the difference between **ingestion** and **transformation**.

5. An order is cancelled in the app DB at 2pm. The warehouse still shows it as "completed" until the nightly job runs. What trade-off does this illustrate?
